We have been talking about email the collective sends on purpose: occasional member updates, notices
when a system affects subscribers, and a real signup on the site footer. Listmonk is the likely
system — self-hosted and API-friendly — but no one has taken the initiative yet.

This is a **proposed** pitch: weigh in before we treat it as active work. If it goes ahead, it would
pair with the Listmonk system idea and need a taker to drive setup and list hygiene.
