What we do

We build and maintain open infrastructure — identity, communication, decision-making, source control, and the public site itself. Services are collectively owned and run for members and anyone who needs them.

Members write about the work in articles and publish notices when something affects users. The goal is simple: make what we operate understandable from the outside.

Who we are

Datakollektivet is its members — people who build, operate, and steward shared infrastructure together. We are async, distributed, and democratically governed. Content lives in git; the site reflects what we commit.
