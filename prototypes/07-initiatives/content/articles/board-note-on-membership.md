The board speaks here as a group. Some announcements fit a person signing their name; others are
collective statements about how the org works.

This article uses `author_group: board` only — no individual `author`. On the site it shows as by
**Board**, linking to the board section on the members page.

## What we're saying

Membership is intentional. We're async, we're distributed, and we're still figuring out lightweight
habits that scale. The members page lists org groups; Matrix rooms attached to each group are the
main path in for people who want to participate before everything is formalised.

If you're curious, read the group descriptions and join the room that fits — or use the contact form
on Join and say hello.
