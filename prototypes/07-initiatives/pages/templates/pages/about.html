About us

A software collective that builds and maintains open infrastructure for its members — and makes that work legible from the outside.

What we do

We run a set of systems — identity, communication, decision-making, source control, and the site itself. Each system has a team lead, admins, and operational updates so anyone can see who is accountable and what is happening.

Members write about the work in articles, publish short notices when something affects users, and keep their profiles in git alongside the content they maintain.

How we work

We are async and distributed. There is no heavy process handbook — instead, we converge on light shared habits: label work in the forge, write updates when users need to know, and keep system pages honest about what is maintained and what needs help.

Content lives in the repository. Anyone in the collective edits markdown files, review happens in the forge, and the site reflects what is in git.

Who we are

The collective is its members — people who build, operate, and steward the systems together. Profiles carry org roles and group membership alongside per-system accountability.

Members are listed by org group on the members page — including the board, maintainers, and moderators.
