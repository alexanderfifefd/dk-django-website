The collective needs written governance before we can onboard members with confidence. Terms of
service, privacy policy, and a clear membership agreement are not bureaucracy for its own sake — they
set expectations for everyone.

## What we're writing

- **Terms of service** — what users can expect from collective-run services
- **Privacy policy** — how we handle personal data across systems
- **Membership agreement** — obligations and rights for org members

## Where this shows up

Once published, these documents will be linked from About and Join. For now, progress lives in the
collective's working groups and shows up here as articles when there is something worth saying.
