Become a volunteer

Volunteers take on real work — moderation, running systems, driving initiatives. It is a step beyond having an account: you are agreeing to show up.

What volunteering can involve

  • Moderation — helping keep collective spaces usable
  • Technical maintenance — systems the collective operates
  • Initiative driving — owning a goal until it lands or hands off
  • Other stewardship — areas the board or maintainers agree need a named person

You need a collective account first — create one before applying. Membership (voting, fee) is separate — see become a member.
