Create an account

Everyone can register. You do not need to be a member or volunteer first — an account is the shared front door to the tools we run.

What you get

  • Forgejo — browse repos, follow issues, contribute when you are ready
  • Loomio — read discussions (member-only voting is separate)
  • Matrix — join rooms that are open to account holders
  • Other services — access to free-tier collective services as we add them

Account creation is handled through our identity service (SSO). You will pick a handle that works across git, chat, and the site.
