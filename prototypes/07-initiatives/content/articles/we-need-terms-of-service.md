We run software for members and publish a site for everyone. Sooner or later someone will ask: *what are
the rules?* *who is liable for what?* *what happens to my data?*

We don't have a good published answer yet. The terms of service initiative is the placeholder until we do.

## Initiative-only articles

This post links to an initiative and **no system**. That fits work that isn't tied to a single piece of
software — legal copy, process design, governance. Compare with a post about Keycloak federation,
which would link to the `keycloak` system instead, or both system and initiative when the story spans
them.

## What we're not doing here

This is not the terms themselves. Drafting legal text is a separate step — ideally with someone who
wants to own that work. The initiative is `recruiting: open` in content for when we pick up
presentation; the slug and pitch exist so articles and index pages have something to link to.

## If you want to help

Read the initiative pitch, get in touch with the board, or propose a outline on Loomio. The site is
built to make these gaps visible rather than hiding them until a crisis forces the question.
