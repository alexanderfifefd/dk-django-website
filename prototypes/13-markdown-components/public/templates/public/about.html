About us

What we do

We run shared infrastructure — identity, communication, decision-making, source control, and the site itself. Members write about the work in articles and keep operational notices where users can find them.

How we work

We are async and distributed. There is no heavy process handbook — instead, we converge on light shared habits: label work in the forge, write updates when users need to know, and keep our public face honest about what is maintained and what needs help.

Content lives in the repository. Anyone in the collective edits markdown files, review happens in the forge, and the site reflects what is in git.

Who we are

The collective is its members — people who build, operate, and steward shared infrastructure together.
